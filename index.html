FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Join free and look around. It costs nothing right now.

Is it free?

Yes. Grayed Out is free to join right now. It will become a paid community later as it grows.

Do I need experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. The starter course, Your First AI Video in a Day (8 lessons, including video lessons), walks you through your first AI video, and the daily AI news posts are written in plain language that's easy to follow.

What tools will I use?

You'll learn AI tools for video, ads, and automation. For example, there's a walkthrough on how to use Higgsfield AI with a free account, and a tutorial on how a 15-second AI commercial is made. Behind-the-scenes teardowns show the exact steps used to make real AI videos.

What happens when it goes paid?

Grayed Out will become a paid community as it grows. Founding members who invite at least 3 people who join get lifetime free membership, even after it becomes paid.

What's inside right now?

The 8-lesson starter course Your First AI Video in a Day, a weekly challenge (starting with "The 15-Second Local Ad"), behind-the-scenes teardowns, how-to video tutorials, short daily AI news posts, and a supportive community feed to share your work and get feedback.

Coming soon: a prompt library, automation recipes, business case studies, a monthly AI tool roundup, and live calls.
